About Me

Developer Mindset

As a developer, I approach problems with curiosity and persistence. I don't expect to know everything, but I'm committed to figuring things out by breaking challenges into smaller steps, researching effectively, and learning through building. I continuously look for ways to improve both my skills and my thinking. For me, a developer mindset is about staying consistent, being adaptable, and always focusing on creating solutions that are both functional and user-friendly.

Evolution

Evolving as a developer means taking ownership of my growth and consistently pushing my skills forward. I started out mostly self-taught, building my foundation through practice, curiosity, and real projects, which taught me how to learn independently and stay disciplined. As I progressed, I chose to join a bootcamp (CodeSpace Academy) to refine my knowledge, fill in gaps, and gain structured, real-world experience. Evolving, for me, is about continuously learning, adapting, and combining self-driven effort with guided growth to become a more capable and well-rounded developer.

Team Work

Communication and teamwork are essential in development because building software is rarely a solo effort—it requires clear collaboration, shared understanding, and the ability to give and receive feedback. My experience working as a barman helped me develop these skills in a fast-paced, team-driven environment where clear communication and coordination were key to delivering a great customer experience. I learned how to stay calm under pressure, work efficiently with others, and adapt quickly, skills that translate directly into working within a development team, where collaboration and clear communication are just as important as writing good code.
